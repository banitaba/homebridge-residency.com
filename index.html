Our Mission

We bridge people to dignified, affordable places to call home.

HomeBridge Residency provides affordable shared-living homes for independent adults throughout Los Angeles and Orange Counties. Our homes are intended for people who can manage their own daily needs and need access to a stable, respectful place to live.

We welcome referrals from a broad range of organizations and community partners, with residential environments designed to feel like homes rather than institutions.

Our Story

Built around the meaning of home.

HomeBridge Residency was founded by USC School of Architecture graduates who believe that a home is more than four walls and a roof. The spaces in which we live influence comfort, stability, dignity, and belonging.

With an architectural perspective on housing, our goal is to create shared-living environments that are practical, welcoming, thoughtfully maintained, and respectful of the people who live in them.

LA & Orange County focus

HomeBridge is being developed to serve communities across both counties.

Current service scope

HomeBridge Residency is a housing provider. Personal care, clinical care, daily-living assistance, medication support, transportation, meal support, and recuperative care are not currently offered.

FAQ

Is the property address public?

No. HomeBridge Residency does not currently provide personal care, daily-living assistance, clinical care, medication support, transportation, meal support, or recuperative care.
